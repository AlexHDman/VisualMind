## Definition

VisualMind Creative Intelligence Model (CIM) is a human-centered decision framework for creating Digital Assets. CIM applies the permanent decision-making principles defined in `VISUALMIND_PRINCIPLES.md`, structured professional knowledge and the Perception Model to form, confirm, generate and evaluate creative directions.

## Distinguishing Approach

CIM differs from direct prompt-to-generation workflows by treating generation as the result of a prior, explicit decision process rather than the starting point.

Its defining characteristics are:

- application of the constitutional decision principles in `VISUALMIND_PRINCIPLES.md` to creative work;
- use of structured knowledge and designated source documents instead of unstructured, isolated instructions;
- evaluation through a formal human Perception Model, including attention, Gestalt organization, recognition, understanding, emotion, memory and action;
- collaboration with the user through confirmation of a human-readable Creative Summary before generation;
- selection of a contextual visual strategy, including composition, color strategy, typography and emotional design, rather than isolated visual choices;
- separation of methodological knowledge, engineering contracts and generation workflow.
